Leonard Chen

Product Design, Research, Strategy

I'm a design leader who bridges strategy and execution. Over the past decade, I've owned end-to-end design processes at companies like Airwallex, ExpressVPN, and Dyson—from early research and hypothesis formation to shipped products and measurable outcomes.

I specialize in making complexity simple. Whether it's building developer experiences, optimizing conversion flows, or designing embedded finance tools, I bring first-principles thinking and rigorous research to craft solutions that work for both users and the business. I also mentor designers and leverage AI as a partner in rapid prototyping and iteration.
